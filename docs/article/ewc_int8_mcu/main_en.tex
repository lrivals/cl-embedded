\begin{abstract}
We study the porting of an \emph{Elastic Weight Consolidation} (EWC) classifier for predictive maintenance
onto a Cortex-M4 microcontroller (NUCLEO-F439ZI, \SI{256}{\kilo\byte} SRAM, no NPU). Three results are
established under a \textbf{paired} protocol (same seed, same sample order, same normalization) between the
PC run and the board. \textbf{(1)} In FP32, the PC$\leftrightarrow$board parity is \emph{exact} in the
frozen protocol (\num{1.000}) and near-exact online (\numrange{0.963}{0.989}), with an inference + online
update latency $\ll$ \SI{100}{\milli\second} (Gap~2). \textbf{(2)} \emph{Naive} post-training quantization
(PTQ) of the binary EWC head collapses on the board: the F1 score drops from $\approx\!\num{0.92}$ to
\numrange{0.05}{0.15}. \textbf{(3)} A bit-exact PC-emulated ablation isolates the cause (\texttt{int8}
accumulator, uncalibrated per-tensor scale) and demonstrates \emph{recovery} through a calibrated kernel:
the \texttt{per\_tensor\_calib} scheme restores $+\!\num{0.88}$ F1 (Pronostia \num{0.9462}, Monitoring
\num{0.9201}), and the \texttt{q15} scheme matches FP32. Weight RAM is divided by~4 (INT8) with no latency
speed-up on the Cortex-M4 FPU --- a \emph{paradox} we acknowledge and tie to a SIMD (CMSIS-NN) direction.
We systematically distinguish \emph{on-board} measurements from \emph{bit-exact PC emulation}, and
explicitly leave "to be measured" the board-v2 campaign cells not yet streamed.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords}: continual learning; EWC; INT8 quantization; PTQ; microcontroller; predictive
maintenance; TinyML.
